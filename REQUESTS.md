## 1. The idea

> So I want to create a program that could look at me studying and be inspired by commisar yarrick make me study possibly joined by other personas
> It will mostly work with pre generated voice with some adjustments (voice can be generated by the eleven labs api).
> But here is the problem, I don't have much AI credits to use it. I am not paying for any API. So I probaly could give it some of the API credits from gemeni but not much. So perfectly that should be the browser extension that reads the page and sees when you get distracted

**Built:** a Manifest V3 browser extension, **Commissar Study Watch**. It is plain JavaScript with no dependencies
and no build step, and this folder is the extension.

- **Watching you study** ([src/sw.js](src/sw.js), [src/core.js](src/core.js)). Start a session from the popup
  and type your subject. The extension then follows:
  - the active tab, its address and title;
  - whether the browser is in focus;
  - keyboard and mouse idle, and screen lock.

  A state machine turns that into events:
  - **distracted**: on a distracting page for more than 5 seconds;
  - **deserted**: still there after 60 seconds, repeated every 2 minutes;
  - **back**: returned to study material;
  - **focusBlock**: every 25 focused minutes;
  - **idle**: no input for 5 minutes;
  - a session verdict at the end: clean, messy, or quit early.

  Time spent asleep or locked is not counted.
- **"Reads the page" without AI.** Pages are judged in this order:
  1. your study and distraction lists;
  2. mixed sites (YouTube and the like), where the title decides: a lecture counts as study;
  3. study words or your subject in the title or address;
  4. optionally, the page's own text, scored locally in the browser.

  Pages none of these can decide are "not judged", so they cost you nothing.
- **Personas.** Your two existing voice packs load as they are: `commissar/` (57 clips) and `helldiver/`
  (Democracy Officer). Pick one, or **Squad** mode, where every line comes from a random member. Adding a persona
  means adding a folder and listing it in `packs.json`.
- **Pre-generated voice with adjustments.** Clips play in an offscreen audio page ([src/offscreen.js](src/offscreen.js)).
  The **gravel** effect from SciEdu-Studio is included: the voice is slowed and deepened, soft-clipped, boosted
  in the bass and the "bark" range, and compressed. You set it from 0 to 3 per persona.
- **Captions.** All 57 Commissar clips were matched back to their text ([captions/commissar.json](captions/commissar.json)),
  so the on-screen banner shows what is being said.
- **ElevenLabs.** [tools/gen-voice.mjs](tools/gen-voice.mjs) records lines with your own key, once. It is a port
  of SciEdu's Python generator.
  - `--dry-run` counts the characters without calling anything; `--max-chars` is a spending cap.
  - Clips already recorded are never paid for again.
  - [tools/lines/commissar.json](tools/lines/commissar.json) adds 41 new Commissar lines written for this tool:
    distracted, deserted, back, idle, focus block, session end, quitting early, partial credit, all done.
  - Recording every missing clip costs about 2,400 characters; the free plan gives about 10,000 a month.
- **Little or no AI credit.** No AI is needed at all. Gemini ([src/gemini.js](src/gemini.js)) is optional and
  **off by default**. When on, it only asks about pages nothing else could judge, after you have stayed on them
  for 20 seconds. It is limited to 40 calls a day, caches each answer for a week, and never retries on its own. It
  sends the title, site and path, and the start of the page text only if you opt in.

## 4. No API at all, reading right and wrong answers

> Is it possible to have no api mode at all. Like when it extracts the right or wrong answer from the browser using the parcing script
> Demo site is in the folder

**Built:** No-API mode is the default.

[src/answers.js](src/answers.js) and [src/watch.js](src/watch.js) read homework pages. The built-in **WebAssign**
rule was worked out from your saved pages and reads three things:
- each question box's **Total** score (`1/1`, `0/1`, `–/1`), not a single part's score;
- its submission counts, added up over the question's parts;
- the check and cross marks.

The page is snapshotted on load and again after every change. Comparing the snapshot with the one before you
pressed Submit gives the result: **quizPass** (right), **quizFail** (wrong), **quizPartial** (partly right) or
**allDone** (every question correct).

Checked on your saved page in a browser: it found all 16 question boxes and read their scores and submission
counts. A simulated right answer gave `quizPass`, a wrong one `quizFail`. Answers also count toward the session
stats (right/wrong in the popup).

## 5. Teaching new sites, with hand check, and identifying study pages

> Also if possible add the filter addition script that lets you add your website file in extension and let it extract the right and wrong with posibility of hand check. Or make it do it by default with new page identifying what is that is that study

**Built:**
- **Teach a site** (Settings → Right and wrong answers, [src/teach.js](src/teach.js)).
  1. Pick a saved page (`.html`) plus its `.css` files.
  2. The extension proposes:
     - the site, from its links;
     - which marks mean right, wrong or partial, from class names and labels in the page and its stylesheets;
     - the repeated question box;
     - the score and submission cells.
  3. **Hand check:** set each mark to right, wrong, partial or ignore, and edit any selector.
  4. **Test** runs the rule on the saved page and shows what it reads.
  5. **Save** stores the rule and asks for access to that site only.

  The saved page is parsed without running its scripts. On your WebAssign page the detector found by itself the
  same question box, score cell and submission cell as the built-in rule, and from its stylesheets the real mark
  classes (`.waMark.mCorrect`, `.mIncorrect`, `.mPartial`), with no layout noise.
- **By default on new pages:** two optional switches, available once you allow the extension on all sites.
  - **Look for right / wrong marks on every site** uses common names such as `is-correct`, `aria-label="Incorrect"`
    and `data-correct`.
  - **Read each page's own text** scores study words, your subject's words, math formulas and "Submit answer"
    forms locally. A page that clearly looks like study counts as study. This can only ever say "study", never
    "distraction", so a wrong guess can't punish you.

## 13. Publishing it safely

> wait i will create the repo in a sec

> https://github.com/alexbespik/Commissar here is the repo make sure its legaly and code vise safe

**Done:**
- **Legal**
  - The code is **AGPL-3.0**, the same licence as SciEdu-Studio, which the audio chain and the generator come
    from.
  - **No voice clips go into the public repo.** The Commissar clips are ElevenLabs output: the free plan is
    non-commercial and asks for credit. The Democracy Officer clips are a personal recording. SciEdu-Studio
    already kept its packs out of git for the same reason.
  - The repo carries the line texts and the generator, so anyone can record their own. With no pack installed,
    the extension speaks with the browser's voice.
  - The README states that the project is not affiliated with Games Workshop, Arrowhead/Sony, Cengage, Google
    or ElevenLabs.
  - The "Commissar" persona is a generic stern officer. Its lines are original, and no franchise names, quotes
    or art are used. Franchise names appear only in your quoted requests, the non-affiliation notice and the local pack name
    `helldiver`.
- **Code safety:** a security review and a correctness review were run, and everything they found was fixed.
  - **Settings messages:** the Settings page's messages were ignored, because Chrome gives it a tab, so the
    service worker took it for a web page. Messages are now told apart by origin.
  - **Locked storage:** web-page scripts can no longer read the extension's storage (the Gemini key, history).
    They ask the service worker for the few settings they need.
  - **No full addresses stored:** page verdicts and answer snapshots are kept by page path or by a hash, never
    the full address with its query string.
  - **Caps:** answer reports are size-capped and limited to one reaction every 4 seconds per tab.
  - **Gemini:** never asked about mail, sign-in or banking pages. The page-text snippet is sent only if it comes
    from the page being judged.
  - **"Teach a site":** reads at most 1 MB of CSS in one linear pass, so a hostile file can't hang it.
  - **Smaller fixes:** the first distraction warning is never held back. "Distraction" works on a subdomain of
    a study site. The fallback voice runs when a clip fails, and Silence stops it.
  - **Token guard:** `npm run check` fails if any file git would commit contains a login token. The saved
    WebAssign pages contain your session; they are git-ignored.

**Done:** The 4.7 Optimization page, already graded, showed two things the empty demo could not:
- **Parts:** WebAssign splits a question into parts, each with its own score and submission count. The reader

  counted as right. It now reads the question's Total cell and adds up the submissions across parts.

## 15. Only fresh results

> and also it should react only to new chandges not to the random stuff on the board

> like if you just failed or passed question it reacts it doesent care about previous

**Built:**
- **Per question:** each question you just submitted is judged on its own new score. Right means pass, even if
  it was wrong before. Wrong means fail. Nothing else on the page counts.
- **Opening a page is silent:** grades already on a page when you open it are a silent baseline. So is coming
  back to an assignment later. While the page is open, every new grade counts, however long you took. A reload
  after Submit counts too, in the same tab within 3 hours, or any tab within 10 minutes.
- **Late redraws are ignored:**
  - The first look waits until the page has been quiet for 1.5 seconds.
  - A snapshot showing *less* grading than the last one (a page still drawing) is ignored.
  - So reloads and re-renders never cheer or scold.
- **Rate limit:** at most one answer reaction every 4 seconds per tab.

## 16. Screen effects, punishments, returning to the study tab, lost attention

> also add ability to the extension to do the additional effects on the screen and control browser to return person to the needed tab
> Also should show punishment from the commisar and see if browser lost the attention (also tell me first how long do you keep the cash like when conversation unloads and cost more )

**Answered first:** the prompt cache.
- **Lifetime:** this session caches the conversation for 1 hour. Each reply restarts the timer and reads it back at
  about 0.05× the normal input price.
- **After more than an hour away:** the next message writes the whole conversation to cache again at 2× the normal
  input price. That one turn's input costs about 40× a cache hit, and it gets more expensive as the conversation
  grows.
- **Agents start with no cache.** The review agents (researcher, architect, reviewer, overseer) each re-read the
  files from scratch, which makes them the most expensive part of each feature.

**Built:** **Discipline levels**, configurable in Settings: off, lenient, standard (the default) and harsh.

| Moment | What happens |
|---|---|
| Distracted | A red flash with the warning |
| Deserted (still on the page after 60 s) | The page greys out and shakes; a countdown, "Returning you to your post in 10 s", then switches you back to the tab you were last studying on |
| Repeated desertion (strike 3, or the 2nd desertion on harsh) | The **Commissar's judgment** lockout: the page is covered until you type the penance line |
| Browser lost attention (another app in front for 2 min) | Voice line, plus a system notification once allowed; on harsh it also tries to bring the browser forward |
| Back in the browser | The "returned" line |

The lockout can never trap you:
- It lifts by itself (30 s by default, never more than 60 s).
- Esc lifts it after 10 seconds, wherever focus is.
- Ending the session or setting discipline to Off removes it at once.
- It is never shown on a study tab, never while you are in another app, and never renewed while a return is
  already pending.

What the browser control will and won't do:
- It never closes, opens or navigates tabs. It only switches to your existing study tab, and only if that tab still
  counts as study.
- Going back on your own cancels the forced return.
- **Back to study tab** in the popup does the same switch on demand.

The popup now also shows the discipline level, punishments so far and minutes away.

## 17. "It doesn't react"

> and now it doesent do anything at distruction

> there was one right answer and commisar didnt react

**Fixed:**
- **Voice lines silently dropped:** a security check added earlier made the audio player reject messages that
  carried no sender address. Chrome doesn't always fill that address in for messages from the extension's own
  background script, so voice lines could be dropped silently. The player now accepts them.
- **Unknown sites never reacted to:** they counted as "not judged" forever. They now count as a distraction after
  5 minutes on the same page (configurable).
- **Diagnostics:** the popup shows a **Last reaction** line: what triggered, and whether the voice played or why it
  failed.
- **Answers judged per part:** WebAssign questions come in parts. The reader judged the whole question, so one
  right part out of five would have played a *wrong-answer* line. It now judges the part you just submitted: right
  means pass, wrong means fail. If WebAssign only adds the check mark, the mark decides. Checked on your saved
  graded page.
- **Page script after a reload:** reloading the extension leaves already open tabs with a dead page script. The
  extension now puts a fresh one into open WebAssign tabs (or every tab, with all-sites access).
- **Regression test:** an end-to-end test runs the background script against a fake browser: start a session,
  open Reddit, check that the voice line and banner go out, then check the countdown and the return to the study
  tab.
