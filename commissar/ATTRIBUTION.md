# Commissar voice clips

The `.mp3` files in this folder were generated with **ElevenLabs** (https://elevenlabs.io) text-to-speech, stock voice
"Daniel" (`onwK4e9ZLuTAKqWW03F9`), model `eleven_multilingual_v2`. The voice is ElevenLabs' own premade voice, not a
clone of any real person. The line texts are original writing (`tools/lines/commissar.json`, `captions/commissar.json`).

Use them for non-commercial purposes and keep this credit with them. Lines generated under an ElevenLabs free plan are
for non-commercial use with attribution to ElevenLabs; commercial use needs a paid ElevenLabs plan for the account that
generates them. You can always record your own with `tools/gen-voice.mjs` and your own key.
