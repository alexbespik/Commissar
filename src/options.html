Commissar Study Watch: settings

Everything works with no AI and no paid API. Voices are the recorded packs in this extension's folder.

Voices

Discipline

How the persona punishes drifting. Nothing is ever closed: a burned tab becomes the condemned page (Back restores it), and you are switched back to a tab you were studying on.

Notifications reach you when another app is in front; macOS Focus or notification settings can hide them. Bringing the browser forward is best effort: the system may refuse to let it jump in front of another app.

Camera watch (on this computer only)

During a session the camera is checked about once a second, right here on your computer, for a phone in your hands and for you at the desk. No picture is ever saved or sent anywhere: only "phone yes/no, person yes/no" reaches the extension. The camera light stays on while a session runs. A phone held below the desk, out of the camera's view, cannot be seen.

Looking down while you type counts as working. Looking down with the keyboard and mouse untouched looks like a phone in your lap: writing on paper, take an I need outside resources pass first, and none of this counts. A second monitor to the side reads as "away": turn this off then.

Keep the camera allowed: during a session the camera runs in his watch window when you post him (it can ask there), or else in the background, which works only when the camera is allowed for good. If Brave asks how long to remember the camera, pick the longest.

A phone in view: a strike, the red flash and he points at you. Still on it 30 s later: he fires, and it counts as a desertion (the next one brings the writing punishment). No keyboard or mouse, but you are at the desk and off your phone: you are reading, not idle.

Mac helper: over fullscreen apps, and which app you are in

A browser cannot show anything over a fullscreen app, nor see which app you switched to. A small helper app on your Mac can: it shows the screamers on top of every app and every Space (nothing of his stays on screen in between), and tells the extension the name of the app in front (nothing else: no window titles, no screen contents). Then a study app (a PDF reader, Anki, your notes) counts as studying, and a distraction app (Telegram, Discord, a game) brings the out-of-browser punishments after a few seconds.

Optional: Gemini for pages nothing else can judge

    
Off by default. Only asks about a page that is on no list, has no study words, and you stayed on for a while; at most the daily cap; each answer is remembered for a week. Sends the page title, site and path (and the start of the page text only if you tick it). Google's free tier may use what you send to improve its products.

    
    

      

      

      

      

    

    

      

    

    
    
    

  


  

    
Sites the AI learned

    
With Gemini on and a key saved, every new site you open is sorted once (after 4 s on it): study, distraction or neutral, with a short reason. It is asked about the site, not each page, so a site costs one call ever. Your own lists above always win, and the sites already on them are never asked about. Change a verdict here and it is kept as yours. Tick "send the start of the page text" above to let it read the page too.
